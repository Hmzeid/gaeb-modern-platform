# Executive Meeting — Agenda & Facilitation Pack (45–60 min)

**Objective (state it in the first minute):** leave with (1) a
date for the one-day decision workshop, (2) named charter-review
owners, (3) named pilot mobilization responsibilities.

| Time | Segment | Lead | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0–5 | Introduction & objectives | Veebase CEO | The three outcomes above; distribution of the package |

## Presenter speaking notes
- Say "synthetic data" before the first screenshot and whenever
  a figure appears. Never let a demo number sound like GAEB's.
- Lead with the refusal moments — they are the governance story
  executives remember: the system declining their data without
  their authorization.
- Do not promise dates for GAEB-side actions; propose, record.
- If a question reaches beyond evidence, say what would prove
  it and where it sits in the plan (workshop / pilot week).

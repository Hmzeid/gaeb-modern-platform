# 11 — Executive Demonstration Guide

Environment: the **demonstration environment** (synthetic data,
clearly labeled). Runtime ≈ 25 minutes. Every figure shown comes
from the live application — if a number looks odd, it is the demo
data talking, and the presenter says so.

1. **Open on the Portfolio Overview (EN).** One screen: registry
   scale, tender pipeline by stage, certificate queues, budget
   posture. Point out the context chips — the numbers are labeled
   with their scope. Switch to **Arabic** live: the whole shell
   mirrors, numerals localize, identifiers stay Latin. (2 min)
2. **Geographic Intelligence.** The dark command view: official
   boundaries for all 27 governorates, coverage honest at 4/27 for
   the demo data, attribution visible. Click a governorate → the
   School Map opens filtered to it. (3 min)
3. **School Map.** Search an Arabic name; sort; page. Mention:
   server-side throughout — the same screen was load-tested at one
   million buildings (Phase 9 evidence). Open a building drawer. (3 min)
4. **Purchases & Contracts.** Create a tender, advance it one stage;
   try to skip a stage — the machine refuses. Filter the register
   by stage. Open the tender drawer: history is the audit log. (4 min)
5. **Technical Abstracts.** Open a certificate drawer: item lines,
   qty × price valuation, deductions. Advance a certificate and
   note the separation of duties — the certifier cannot authorize
   payment; the suite tests that refusal. (4 min)
6. **Migration Center.** The batch from the synthetic import:
   6 records, 3 loaded, 3 quarantined — open the rejects and show
   the raw evidence; open the reconciliation report and read the
   three columns aloud: technical outcome, business equivalence,
   customer acceptance. That separation is the product's honesty
   model. Show an approval being granted and revoked. (5 min)
7. **Close on gates, not features.** Show §12 of this package:
   engineering verified; pilot ready; acceptance and production
   are GAEB's decisions, with the eight business decisions in §06
   as the immediate next step. (2 min)

Do not demonstrate: real-data migration (none exists), the Docker
path (unverified), or any BD-dependent behavior as final.
